Blokbay Crypto Payments

Accept USDT, USDC, ETH, BTC and SOL in your Odoo eCommerce and on invoices

No custody: funds never pass through us

The buyer pays on a secure Blokbay page, from a connected wallet or by address and QR code from any wallet or exchange. Paid from a wallet, each payment is split in the same transaction: you receive 99% straight in your wallet, Blokbay keeps 1% of the amount paid. Paid by address, the buyer makes two transfers to exact amounts, one of them to your address (Bitcoin always works this way); on Base, orders from 10 USD can also be paid with one transfer to a one-time payment address, a public contract with no owner that forwards your share to your wallet. No deposit that can be stolen, no frozen funds, no waiting for a payout.

Coins and networks

  • USDT and USDC on Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, Arbitrum, Polygon and Solana; USDC on Base.
  • ETH on Ethereum, Base and Arbitrum; SOL on Solana; Bitcoin (BTC).
  • The buyer can change coin and network before paying; a confirmation page opens as soon as the payment arrives.

Pay customer invoices with Blokbay

  • On a posted customer invoice, Pay with Blokbay creates a payment link in USDC or USDT with the same lines, taxes and total.
  • The link is shown on the invoice, printed in the invoice PDF and added to the invoice email sent from Odoo.
  • When your client pays, the signed webhook registers the payment and the invoice is marked paid. 1% per paid invoice, no fixed costs.

How it works

  • The buyer chooses Crypto (USDT, USDC, ETH, BTC, SOL) at checkout and is redirected to the Blokbay payment page.
  • When the payment is confirmed on the blockchain the transaction is done and the order is confirmed. The network, the coin and the blockchain transaction are saved on the payment transaction.
  • A signed webhook updates the transaction even if the buyer closes the page; the return page checks the payment with the API.
  • Payments that expire are cancelled. A payment confirmed after the expiry is still recorded, because the funds already reached you.

Setup in 3 minutes

  1. Open a free Blokbay account at blokbay.com and add your wallet.
  2. In Payments from your site copy the public key, the secret key and the webhook signing secret.
  3. In Odoo open Invoicing / Website > Configuration > Payment Providers > Blokbay, paste the keys, copy the webhook URL into Blokbay and set the provider to Enabled.
